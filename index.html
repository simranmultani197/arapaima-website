How a job runs

One crew from the first site visit to the day the sidewalk is clear again. Call us at any stage and we'll pick it up from there.

  2. Step 1

    Site evaluation

    We visit the building, measure the frontage and look at what the work above will need.

  3. Step 2

    Drawings & permits

    Structural drawings and permit support, so the shed or scaffold is designed for your site and approved to go up.

  4. Step 3

    Installation

    Our crew puts up the sidewalk shed first to protect the public, then builds the scaffold on schedule.

  5. Step 4

    Support during work

    Hoists, debris chutes and adjustments as your project moves, with safety kept front and center.

  6. Step 5

    Dismantle

    When the job is done we take everything down and leave the sidewalk clear.
